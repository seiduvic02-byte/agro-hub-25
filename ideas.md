# Agro Hub — Design Direction

## Direction

A warmer, more editorial evolution of the supplied Agro Hub marketplace. Treat the supplied HTML’s existing forest-green, warm-ivory, pale-leaf palette, agricultural photography, serif display headlines, and plainspoken Nigerian marketplace copy as the source identity. Make the experience feel like a trusted produce market—not a finance dashboard or a generic farm SaaS template.

## Design dimensions

- **Design movement:** Contemporary editorial commerce, grounded in the source’s nature-forward palette and direct farmer-to-buyer purpose.
- **Core principles:** Let produce and people lead. Keep discovery and price/quantity information easy to scan. Use clear trust cues without pretending to verify a seller. Give key actions a visible, immediate response. Treat all supplied example listings and original counts as illustrative rather than live activity.
- **Color philosophy:** Deep evergreen for navigation, primary actions and important status; fresh leaf green for active selection; warm ivory and soft oat for page surfaces; muted sage for secondary surfaces; crisp paper-white product cards; dark ink and quiet gray-green text. Preserve accessible contrast and use amber sparingly for pending states.
- **Layout paradigm:** A generous editorial landing page with a tight sticky navigation, asymmetrical hero, one visual focal point, a straightforward browse/filter bar, orderly product cards, spacious story/trust sections and a compact footer. Workspace views use a compact, readable side-navigation and table/card hybrids, collapsing to a simple mobile menu and stacked content.
- **Signature elements:** A memorable crop/field mark, pill-shaped category filters, listing cards with location/availability/price visible at a glance, small verification/status labels with explicit meaning, and a persistent path back to “Explore produce.”
- **Interaction philosophy:** Search and filters react immediately; forms show validation, errors and confirmations; modal dialogs have clear close/escape behavior; transactions never claim payment succeeded without provider confirmation. Avoid fake dashboard counts, fake seller verification and unlabelled demo inventory.
- **Animation:** Use restrained 160–240 ms easing for hover, focus and disclosure states. No scroll-jacking or decorative motion that competes with browsing. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and retain visible focus rings.
- **Typography system:** Playfair Display (or the source’s existing serif choice) for editorial headlines; DM Sans for navigation, body copy, labels, prices and controls. Use a small, consistent type scale and tabular numerals for Naira prices and quantities.
- **Brand essence:** Direct-from-farm, dependable, practical, contemporary, Nigerian.
- **Brand voice:** Clear and helpful, warm but not sentimental. Prefer concrete terms such as “Available this week,” “Request quantity,” and “Payment not connected” over unsupported claims like “guaranteed,” “verified,” or “secure payment.”
- **Wordmark/logo:** “Agro Hub” wordmark in a straightforward sans-serif alongside a bold, standalone field-and-leaf emblem. The required project logo asset will be an opaque, full-bleed 1:1 square PNG with a simple three-shape agricultural silhouette, generous same-color clearance, no text, no built-in rounded corners, and no mockup.
- **Signature brand color:** Deep harvest evergreen.

## Asset placements

1. One original, photoreal hero image of a smallholder farmer among fresh produce in a recognizably Nigerian agricultural setting; compose with clear visual breathing room for an adjacent headline, without embedded text or visible brand marks.
2. One project-specific, flat geometric field-and-leaf emblem following the logo specification above. Use this file for the site favicon and Dashboard/Preview logo metadata, while the header may use a separate small mark if the opaque background does not suit its treatment.
